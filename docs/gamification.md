Status: ACTIVE source contract. Verification: OPEN / IN PROGRESS. This describes the current local implementation, not a deployment or runtime acceptance.

## Rewards and ranks

XP and course credits use integer millionths. Each eligible event receives base XP and a bonus calculated from the rank before that event; a rank earned by it affects later events. LP is a separate integer currency earned through badge tiers and missions and spent on cosmetics. Rank art and achievement badge art are distinct.

| Rank | Total XP minimum | Course credits minimum | Future XP bonus | Zyra bonus |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Rakyat | 0 | 0 | 0% | 0% |
| Kades | 1,000 | 0 | 0% | 0% |
| Camat | 10,000 | 1 | 5% | 5% |
| Bupati | 30,000 | 3 | 10% | 10% |
| Gubernur | 60,000 | 6 | 15% | 15% |
| Presiden | 100,000 | 10 | 20% | 25% |

Rakyat is the starting status, and Kades is the first earned rank. Both XP and course credit thresholds must be met. The standard student Zyra provider limit starts at 15 turns; applying each rank percentage and flooring gives 15, 15, 15, 16, 17, and 18. The first eligible allowance read fixes the rank snapshot for the Jakarta day; the next Jakarta day permits a new snapshot. Existing free tutoring access remains unchanged.

An approved checklist allocates a reference course pool over stable item IDs. Article and explanation categories each allocate up to 1,000 XP at 10 items; questions allocate up to 6,000 at 300 items, and flashcards up to 2,000 at 200. Smaller categories receive a proportional pool; larger categories share the capped pool. A completed checklist contributes up to one course credit, proportional to its possible XP out of 10,000. Allocation and credits retain micro-unit precision.

Six achievement families each have five tiers, Bronze through Emerald. Thresholds in tier order are:

Tier awards grant 25, 50, 100, 200, and 400 LP respectively. The local catalog contains 28 items across five slots, priced from 250 to 2,000 LP. Existing IDs and prices are preserved. The last verified staging catalog contains seven items; migration 0016, migration 0017, and the expanded catalog seed remain pending.

## Evidence and eligibility

Admins prepare and approve versioned course checklists. A student's first eligible learning start freezes the approved version. Approval rejects a stale source inventory. Reward processing checks the current published source and version against the frozen item and receipt, so a later source version change can fail closed for that item; automatic migration of a student's frozen checklist is not promised. No historical learning awards are backfilled.

Article completion requires evidence for the relevant sections. Explanation completion is an explicit action for the full collection. Rewardable quiz sources are published, quiz-eligible objective multiple-choice questions with server-held canonical question, course, version, options and answer provenance. Daily and standard submission, reward facts, and final tutor grading share the same server-side correctness rule; essays remain subject to manual review. Exposure history initializes conservatively from prior attempts, records disclosures including submission scores, and permits a qualifying retry only on a later Jakarta day after exposure. A submission result is an immutable causal boundary written in the same transaction as its fact. Disclosure and submission transactions serialize per viewer before question locks, and replay checks every earlier disclosure by persisted order, including equal-millisecond events. The result boundary itself and later events are excluded. Within that boundary, the greatest disclosure timestamp is used conservatively if clocks move backwards. A causally earlier disclosure with a future wall time is preserved and denies the reward without blocking submission. Staff answer, correctness and score views, including authorized authoring tools, are recorded for the authenticated viewer. Authorized learner reviews also retain unresolved compatibility-bank IDs; these disclosures are reconciled when a canonical question appears later. Reconciliation uses a set-based insert with stable identities, including disclosures recorded after initialization. A tryout snapshot whose source question no longer exists is conservatively treated as a course-wide disclosure until a later Jakarta day. Pre-migration pending facts without a new-format boundary fail closed. Uncertain or unsupported history fails closed. Flashcard XP requires two eligible recalls of the same card, with the second at least seven days after the first accepted recall; eligible recalls must also be due and have a qualifying grade.

Receipts and learning facts feed a durable outbox and processor with idempotent ledger and account projections. Mission eligibility uses verified events. Selected leaderboard and profile views use bounded projections and selected-tab reads rather than rebuilding full reward history. Quiz submission batches exposure initialization, latest-exposure reads, facts, receipts and outbox inserts. Course access and checklist-start work is reused per distinct course. Outbox processing uses an indexed candidate window and a global limit of 100 events per invocation. A full successful batch schedules a continuation, with one worker at a time; the five-minute cron remains recovery. A persistently failing user can still delay another user within that window; no latency guarantee or load benchmark is established.

## Surfaces, privacy, and assets

The approved feature is always on, with no gamification flag. Source routes include `/achievements`, `/profile/edit`, public member profiles under `/students/`, `/leaderboard` with week, month, all-time and following views, course-scoped academic rankings, and `/admin/gamification`. The global leaderboard is an explicitly approved participation ranking based on base XP, not an academic ranking. Academic student rankings remain course-scoped.

Profiles default to private. Member visibility, leaderboard participation, following and optional profile fields use their respective settings and consent. Users can block and report. The student photo remains the avatar identity. Owned cosmetic frames can be selected around it; up to three earned badges can be featured separately.
