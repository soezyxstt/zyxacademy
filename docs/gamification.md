## Rewards and ranks

XP and course credits use integer millionths. Each eligible event receives base XP and a bonus calculated from the rank before that event; a rank earned by it affects later events. LP is a separate integer currency earned through badge tiers, missions and eligible discussion answers and spent on cosmetics. Rank art and achievement badge art are distinct.

Discussion rewards use the existing outbox, account lock and idempotent ledgers: 5 base XP per eligible question and 10 base XP plus 1 LP per eligible answer to another person's question, capped at five questions and ten answers per Jakarta day. A student can receive each reward kind once per thread. Staff, self-answers, replies, forwards and edits are excluded. Discussion rewards do not create course credits or learning evidence. The local implementation and remaining integration and runtime limits are recorded in [discussions](discussions.md).

## Evidence and eligibility

The admin Gamification page and sidebar entry have been removed. Server-side checklist preparation and approval helpers remain, along with existing approved checklists and student rewards. A student's first eligible learning start freezes the approved version. Approval rejects a stale source inventory. Reward processing checks the current published source and version against the frozen item and receipt, so a later source version change can fail closed for that item; automatic migration of a student's frozen checklist is not promised. No historical learning awards are backfilled.

Article completion requires evidence for the relevant sections. Explanation completion is an explicit action for the full collection. Rewardable quiz sources are published, quiz-eligible objective multiple-choice questions with server-held canonical question, course, version, options and answer provenance. Idea mastery evidence also requires this server-held identity and the matching canonical prompt; historical snapshots without version provenance are graded and retained but do not produce Idea evidence. Legacy essay reviews remain available without attributing them to current Idea links. Daily and standard submission, reward facts, and final tutor grading share the same server-side correctness rule; essays remain subject to manual review. Exposure history uses an indexed maximum-time projection per viewer, course and question, plus course-wide wildcard entries for unresolved disclosures. Submission takes the viewer lock before question locks and captures an immutable observation of the prior evidence before disclosing its own result. Replay verifies that observation against the fact, receipt, owned snapshot and canonical question. Later disclosures and later canonical publication cannot change it. An ambiguous equal-time legacy disclosure or a future disclosure timestamp denies the reward without blocking grading. Staff answer, correctness and score views, including authorized authoring tools, are recorded for the authenticated viewer. Learner submission and compatibility reviews retain missing, draft or invalid-provenance snapshot question IDs under the course derived from the owned attempt, and unmappable tryouts use a course wildcard. A compact reverse index of pre-cutover attempts preserves conservative legacy initialization, including malformed snapshots; absence is trusted only after the guarded backfill. Old pending facts without an observation fail closed. The migration and backfill must finish before this always-on behavior is deployed, as described in the deployment guide. Flashcard XP requires two eligible recalls of the same card, with the second at least seven days after the first accepted recall; eligible recalls must also be due and have a qualifying grade. The primary isolated component visual check passed 36 states; full application runtime, font metrics and staging remain open.

Receipts and learning facts feed a durable outbox and processor with idempotent ledger and account projections. Mission eligibility uses verified events. Selected leaderboard and profile views use bounded projections and selected-tab reads rather than rebuilding full reward history. Quiz submission batches exposure initialization, latest-exposure reads, facts, receipts and outbox inserts. Course access and checklist-start work is reused per distinct course. Outbox processing uses an indexed candidate window and a global limit of 100 events per invocation. A full successful batch schedules a continuation, with one worker at a time; the five-minute cron remains recovery. A persistently failing user can still delay another user within that window; no latency guarantee or load benchmark is established.
